Our Story

A home on campus for people who love South Asian film.

The Indian Cinema Collective brings Bruins together around Indian and South Asian films in every language. We host small, cozy screenings, bigger community nights, outings across LA, and plenty of socials where you don't need to have seen the movie to join in.

Whether you grew up on Sunday-afternoon Doordarshan reruns or you're about to watch your first Bollywood film, there's a seat for you.
